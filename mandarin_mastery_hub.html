Mastering the 'ü' Sound

Lesson 2.4

Expressing Daily Activities & Time

Dialogue 1: Daily Routine

A: 你几点起床？ (Nǐ jǐ diǎn qǐchuáng?)

What time do you get up?

B: 我早上七点起床。 (Wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.)

I get up at 7:00 in the morning.

Grammar Rule: Time Word Placement

In Mandarin Chinese, time words (like 早上 zǎoshang - morning, 七点 qī diǎn - 7 o'clock) almost always appear before the verb or right after the subject.

[Subject] + [Time] + [Verb] + [Object]
